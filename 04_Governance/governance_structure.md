# Governance Structure

## Fictional Northstar AI Governance Model

### AI Governance Committee

Cross-functional approval and oversight body.

**Members represented:**
- Risk
- Legal / Privacy
- Technology
- Customer Experience
- Internal Audit

**Core responsibilities:**
- approve new AI use cases before pilot and general release;
- determine governance requirements;
- review material incidents;
- review residual risk;
- accept or reject deployment conditions.

### Business Owner

**Role:** Head of Customer Experience

Accountable for business performance, operational outcomes, and the agent's approved purpose.

### AI Risk Owner

**Role:** Risk function

Accountable for ongoing AI risk monitoring, escalation, risk-register maintenance, and residual-risk reporting.

### Supporting functions

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Technology | Architecture, permissions, deployment controls and change management |
| Security | AI-specific security threats and adversarial testing |
| Legal / Privacy | Applicable legal and privacy analysis |
| Internal Audit | Independent verification of governance controls |
| Procurement | Third-party AI vendor due diligence |
| Customer Experience | Operational quality and escalation workflows |

## Accountability principle

A policy or committee does not replace individual accountability. Every production AI system must have named operational and risk owners.
